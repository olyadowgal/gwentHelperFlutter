Sidebar + stats column + card rows, all horizontal. Forces landscape orientation. Closest to Android. Works best on tablets and landscape phones.

B — Portrait-adaptive

Players on top, weather row, then card rows — all vertical. Works on any phone orientation. Stats inline with each row. Sacrifices the Android sidebar look.

💡 Note: Option A requires locking the game screen to landscape. Option B works in portrait and landscape.
